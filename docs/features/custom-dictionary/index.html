How do I make transcription spell names and technical terms correctly?

Add the words to Murmur's custom dictionary. Murmur gives these terms to Whisper as vocabulary hints when it transcribes, which makes it more likely to spell them your way. You can also add a word straight from Find and replace after correcting it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the dictionary fix old transcripts?

It applies to new transcriptions. Use Find and replace to fix words in notes that are already transcribed.

Does iCloud sync include the dictionary?

Yes, when you turn on iCloud sync your dictionary words sync along with your notes and folders.
