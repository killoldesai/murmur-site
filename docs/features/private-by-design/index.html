Is there a private transcription app that does not upload audio?

Murmur is built for exactly that. It transcribes with Whisper on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and never uploads your audio or transcripts. The only network use is the one-time model download, App Store purchases and optional iCloud sync to your own private iCloud.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Murmur upload my recordings?

No. Recordings and transcripts are processed and stored on your device. We have no server to receive them.

Is Murmur suitable for confidential conversations?

Because audio never leaves the device, Murmur suits sensitive work such as interviews, legal notes or patient conversations. Check your own organisation's rules for recording and storing such material.

What network connections does Murmur make?

The one-time speech model download, App Store purchases through StoreKit, and iCloud if you turn on sync. Nothing else.
