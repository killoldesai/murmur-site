How do I transcribe an audio file on my iPhone?

In Murmur, tap Import and choose the file from Files, or a video from Photos. You can also open an audio or video file from another app and choose Murmur. Murmur converts it to the format Whisper needs, adds it to the queue and transcribes it on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

In the app

How it works

Tap Import

Choose Files, or Videos to pick from Photos.

Pick one file or many

Each file joins the queue as its own note.

Get transcripts

Murmur works through the queue on the device and each note appears in your library when it is ready.

Details

What you get

Audio and video

Common audio formats and videos both work. Murmur pulls the soundtrack out of a video and transcribes it.

Long files, small memory

Files are converted in chunks, so a two-hour recording does not overwhelm your device.

A batch queue

Add several files and watch each one move from waiting to transcribing to ready.

Open in Murmur

Murmur registers for audio and video files, so it appears when you share or open them from other apps.

Drag and drop on iPad and Mac

Drop files onto the iPad import screen or anywhere in the Mac window.

Real-time speed on Mac

The Mac queue shows how many times faster than real time each file is transcribing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which file types can Murmur import?

Murmur can import the audio and video formats Apple's frameworks read, such as M4A, MP3, WAV, AIFF, CAF, MP4 and MOV.

Can I transcribe a Voice Memo?

Yes. In Voice Memos, share the recording and choose Murmur, or save it to Files and import it from there.

Can I transcribe a Zoom or Teams recording?

Yes. Save the recording file to your device and import it. Murmur only transcribes the file; it never joins calls.
