What is on-device transcription?

On-device transcription means the speech-to-text model runs on your own phone or computer instead of a company's server. Murmur downloads the Whisper model once, then turns your audio into text locally. The recording is never uploaded, so nobody else can read or keep it.

In the app

How it works

Download the model once

On first use, Murmur downloads the speech model over Wi-Fi. This is the only time it needs the internet to transcribe.

Record or import

Tap record, or bring in audio and video files from Files, Photos or other apps.

Read the transcript

Whisper runs on the device and the note fills with timestamped lines you can play, search and edit.

Details

What you get

Whisper Large v3 Turbo

The recommended model is Whisper Large v3 Turbo in a compressed form, about 632 MB. It is the most accurate choice and still fits comfortably on a phone.

A lighter Small model

On older devices, or when space is tight, pick the Small model (about 500 MB). You can switch models later in Settings.

13 languages and auto-detect

Transcribe English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi or Arabic, or let Murmur detect the language.

Timestamps on every line

Each line of the transcript carries its time in the recording, so you can jump back to exactly where it was said.

Queues work for you

Record several notes in a row or import a batch. Murmur transcribes them one after another and picks up where it left off if the app was closed.

Your words, spelled your way

Add names and jargon to the custom dictionary and Murmur passes them to Whisper as vocabulary hints.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is on-device transcription as accurate as cloud services?

Murmur uses Whisper Large v3 Turbo, the same family of models many cloud services build on. Accuracy depends most on audio quality: a clear recording with one speaker close to the microphone gives the best results. The custom dictionary helps with names and technical terms.

Which devices can run Murmur's transcription?

Murmur runs on iPhone and iPad, and on Macs with Apple silicon. Apple Watch records audio and sends it to your iPhone, which does the transcription.

How much storage does the speech model need?

Large v3 Turbo needs about 632 MB and the Small model about 500 MB. You only need one of them.

Does transcription drain my battery?

Speech recognition is demanding work, so long files use more power than normal app use. Murmur transcribes one note at a time and you can keep using your device while it works.
