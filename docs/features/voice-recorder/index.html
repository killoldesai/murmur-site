What is the best way to record and transcribe a meeting on iPhone?

Open Murmur, tap the record button and place your iPhone near the people speaking. Tap the bookmark button at key moments. When you stop, Murmur transcribes the recording on your iPhone with Whisper, so you get a timestamped transcript without uploading the audio anywhere.

In the app

How it works

Tap record

The waveform moves and the timer runs.

Bookmark key moments

Tap the bookmark when something important is said.

Stop and read

Murmur transcribes on the device and your bookmarks are waiting in the note.

Details

What you get

Bookmarks while you record

Flag a moment with one tap. Bookmarks show up on the timeline and in the transcript so you can find them later.

Pause and resume

Pause during a break and carry on in the same note.

Safe if the app closes

Murmur records in a format that stays readable even if the app is closed mid-recording, and recovers the note at the next launch.

Recorded for Whisper

Audio is captured as 16 kHz mono, the format Whisper reads directly, then compacted to M4A after transcription to save space.

From your wrist or menu bar

Start a recording from Apple Watch, or from the Mac menu bar and a global keyboard shortcut.

Haptics you can feel

A light tap confirms that recording started, so you can trust it without looking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I record for hours?

Yes. Murmur is built for long meetings and lectures. Recording length is limited by your device's free storage.

What happens if my phone locks or the app closes during a recording?

The audio is written in a crash-safe format. If the app is closed mid-recording, Murmur finds the note at the next launch and keeps what was captured.

Can I record on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Murmur watch app records and sends the audio to your iPhone, which transcribes it.
