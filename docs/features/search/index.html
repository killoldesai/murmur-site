Can I search inside my voice recordings?

Yes. Once Murmur has transcribed your recordings, the search screen looks through every transcript and summary at once. It ignores case and accents, shows each matching line with its timestamp, and opens the note at that moment so you can play it.

In the app

How it works

Tap Search in the library

Type a word or phrase.

Pick a result

The note opens at that line.

Replace if needed

Use Find and replace from the note's menu.

Details

What you get

All notes at once

Search every transcript and summary, or narrow to just transcripts or just summaries.

Case and accent insensitive

Search for "cafe" and find "Café".

Timestamps on hits

Each result shows when it was said, so you can jump straight there.

Find and replace

Step through matches in a note and replace one or all of them.

Teach the dictionary

When you replace a misheard word, Murmur can add the correct spelling to your dictionary for next time.

Search on Mac

Press ⌘F in the Mac app to search the whole library.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does search work on free notes?

Yes. Search reads the full text of every note, including the parts beyond the free two-minute preview.

Can I replace a word in every note at once?

Find and replace works inside one note at a time, so you can review each change.
