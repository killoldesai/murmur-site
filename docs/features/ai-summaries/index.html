Can I get AI meeting summaries without uploading my recording?

Yes. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Murmur summarizes the transcript with Apple's on-device model. Choose Meeting, Lecture or Bullet points, and Murmur returns a summary with action items. The transcript stays on the device the whole time.

In the app

How it works

Open a transcribed note

Switch from Transcript to Summary.

Choose a template

Meeting, Lecture or Bullet points.

Read and act

Tick off action items or copy the summary into your notes.

Details

What you get

Three templates

Meeting (decisions and next steps), Lecture (key ideas and terms) or Bullet points (a quick skim).

Action items you can tick

Tasks pulled from the conversation become a checklist saved with the note.

Long recordings handled

Murmur splits long transcripts into parts, summarizes each and then combines them.

Copy or export

Copy the summary in one tap, or include it in a text or Markdown export.

Talk time on iPad and Mac

See how much each labelled speaker talked, beside the summary.

Only where supported

Summaries need a device with Apple Intelligence. On other devices the Summary tab is hidden, and transcripts work as normal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which devices support Murmur summaries?

Summaries use Apple Intelligence, so they need a device and OS version that support it. Murmur hides the Summary tab on devices without it.

Is my transcript sent to an AI service?

No. Summaries are made with Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Murmur does not call any cloud AI service.

Can I edit a summary?

You can copy it anywhere and edit it there, and the action items checklist is saved with the note.
