Optional iCloud sync

Your notes on every device, through your own iCloud

Turn on iCloud sync and your notes, transcripts, summaries, folders and dictionary appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. It uses your private iCloud database, which we cannot read.

Does Murmur sync between iPhone and Mac?

Yes, if you turn it on. Murmur's iCloud sync is off by default. When you switch it on in Settings, notes, transcripts, summaries, folders and dictionary words sync through your own private iCloud database to your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Audio files stay on the device that recorded them.

In the app

How it works

Open Settings

Turn on Sync with iCloud.

Reopen Murmur

Sync starts at the next launch.

Open Murmur on another device

Signed in to the same Apple ID, your notes appear.

Details

What you get

Off until you choose

Sync is opt-in. Murmur works fully on one device without it.

Your private iCloud

Data goes to your own CloudKit private database. There is no Murmur server and we cannot see it.

Transcripts, not audio

Audio stays on the device that recorded it, which keeps your iCloud storage light. Other devices show the transcript.

A backup first

Before sync is first turned on, Murmur saves a local copy of your library.

No double work

A note is only transcribed by the device that has its audio, so devices never fight over it.

Takes effect at next launch

Turn it on, reopen Murmur, and your library starts syncing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does sync upload my audio?

No. Only notes, transcripts, summaries, folders and dictionary words sync. Audio stays where it was recorded.

Can Murmur read my synced notes?

No. They live in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple ID can access.

What if I am not signed in to iCloud?

Murmur keeps working on the device and Settings shows that you need to sign in to sync.
