How do I check and correct a transcript against the audio?

In Murmur, tap any line to play the recording from that point. The current line is highlighted and the view scrolls with playback. Tap Edit to change the text, split a long line, merge short ones or set the speaker. Undo is always one tap away.

What you get

Tap to play

Break a long line in two or join short fragments, then label speakers line by line.

Find and replace

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit a transcript after it is made?

Yes. Edit text, speakers and line breaks at any time. Murmur Pro is needed to edit notes longer than two minutes.

Does editing change the audio?

No. Edits change the transcript only. The recording stays as it was.
