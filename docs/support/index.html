Troubleshooting

Common fixes

The transcript is empty or stuck

Check that the speech model finished downloading in Settings. Transcription runs one note at a time; queued notes wait their turn.

Names are misspelled

Add them to the custom dictionary, then fix existing notes with Find and replace.

The Summary tab is missing

Summaries need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Murmur hides the tab on other devices.

Notes do not appear on my other devices

iCloud sync is off by default. Turn it on in Settings on each device, make sure each is signed in to the same Apple ID, and reopen Murmur.

Apple Watch recordings are not arriving

Open Murmur on your iPhone and keep the watch near it. Recordings wait on the watch and send when the two are connected.
