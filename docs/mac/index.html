Fast, private transcription on Apple silicon. Drop in a folder of recordings, start recording from the menu bar with a shortcut, and export straight to Markdown.

How can I transcribe audio offline on a Mac?

Install Murmur on a Mac with Apple silicon and download the Whisper model once. Then drag audio or video files into the window, or record from the menu bar with Control-Option-R. Murmur transcribes on your Mac and never uploads the audio.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Macs does Murmur support?

Murmur for Mac needs a Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 15 or later.

Can Murmur run without a Dock icon?

Yes. Settings can hide the Dock icon and keep Murmur in the menu bar.
