How much does Murmur cost?

Murmur is free to download and shows the first 2 minutes of every note. Murmur Pro costs $3.99 a month, $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, or $39.99 once for lifetime access. Prices are in US dollars; the App Store shows your local price.

FAQ

Pricing questions

What is free in Murmur?

Everything works on the free version, and every note shows its first 2 minutes. Recording and transcription are not capped; the full transcript is saved, so upgrading later unlocks your older notes. Search, iCloud sync and the Apple Watch app are included for everyone.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial. You are not charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Does one purchase work on iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes. Murmur is a universal purchase tied to your Apple ID.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in Murmur and tap Restore purchases. Murmur also checks your purchases when it starts.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is Family Sharing supported?

Subscriptions and the lifetime plan follow Apple's Family Sharing settings for each product, as configured on the App Store.
