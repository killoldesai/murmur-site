Can I record voice notes on Apple Watch and transcribe them?

Yes. The Murmur watch app records audio on your Apple Watch and sends it to your iPhone, which transcribes it with Whisper on the device. If your phone is out of range, recordings wait on the watch and send when it is back.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the watch transcribe the audio itself?

No. The watch records; your iPhone does the transcription so the watch stays fast and saves battery.

Do I need my iPhone nearby to record?

No. You can record away from your phone and the recordings send when the two are back in range.
