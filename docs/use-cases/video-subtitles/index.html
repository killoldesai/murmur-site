How do I make an SRT subtitle file from a video?

Import the video into Murmur from Photos, Files or by dragging it onto the Mac app. Murmur extracts the audio and transcribes it with Whisper on your device. Check the lines, then choose Share, Subtitles to export an SRT file you can upload to YouTube or add in your video editor.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Murmur burn subtitles into the video?

No. Murmur exports an SRT file; your video editor or YouTube displays it or burns it in.

Does it work for other languages?

Yes, for the 13 languages Murmur supports, or set it to auto-detect.
