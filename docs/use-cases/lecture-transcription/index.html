What is a good app to record and transcribe lectures?

Murmur records lectures on iPhone, iPad or Mac and transcribes them on the device with Whisper, so it works without Wi-Fi. Bookmarks mark key moments, search finds any term across all lectures, and the Lecture summary pulls out the main ideas.

The problem

What gets in the way

  • Lecture halls often have weak Wi-Fi.
  • Handwritten notes miss details.
  • Finding one explanation in hours of audio is slow.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I organise lectures by course?

Yes. Create a folder for each course and file notes into it.

Should I ask before recording a lecture?

Many schools have rules about recording classes. Check your school's policy and ask the lecturer.
