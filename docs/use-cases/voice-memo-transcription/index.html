How can I turn voice memos into text on iPhone?

Record the note in Murmur, or share an existing Voice Memos recording to Murmur. Murmur transcribes it with Whisper on your iPhone and saves it as a searchable note. You can also record on Apple Watch and find the transcript on your iPhone.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I transcribe old Voice Memos?

Yes. Share them to Murmur or save them to Files and import them.

How do I record from my wrist?

Use the Murmur watch app, its complication, or the Action button on supported watches.
