How do journalists transcribe interviews privately?

A private option is to transcribe on the device. With Murmur, you record or import the interview on an iPhone, iPad or Mac and Whisper transcribes it locally. Label the interviewer and the source, tap any line to check the quote against the audio, and export with timestamps.

What gets in the way

  • Uploading a source's voice to a cloud service creates a copy you do not control.
  • Checking quotes against audio takes ages.
  • Paid human transcription is slow and costly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I delete the audio and keep the text?

Yes. Turn on Delete audio after transcribing in Settings, or delete individual notes.

Does Murmur work with external recorders?

Yes. Import the audio files from the recorder through Files or by dragging them onto the Mac app.
