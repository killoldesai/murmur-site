Murmur FAQ

What is Murmur?

Murmur is a speech-to-text app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. It records or imports audio and transcribes it with OpenAI's Whisper model running on your own device. It also summarizes, searches and exports transcripts, with no account and no uploads.

Does Murmur upload my audio?

No. Audio and transcripts are processed and stored on your device. Murmur has no server. If you turn on iCloud sync, notes (not audio) are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot read.

Does Murmur work offline?

Yes. After a one-time download of the speech model on Wi-Fi, recording, transcription, search, editing and export work with no internet connection.

Is Murmur free?

Murmur is free to download. Free notes show the first 2 minutes of each transcript. Murmur Pro unlocks full transcripts, editing, export, find and replace and summaries for every note, including older ones. Pro is $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, $3.99 a month, or $39.99 once for lifetime.

Which languages does Murmur support?

Murmur transcribes English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic, and can auto-detect the language. The app's interface is in English.

How accurate is Murmur?

Murmur uses Whisper Large v3 Turbo, a high-accuracy speech model. Results depend mostly on audio quality. A custom dictionary helps with names and jargon, and you can fix any line in the editor.

Which devices does Murmur run on?

iPhone, iPad, Macs with Apple silicon (macOS 15 or later) and Apple Watch. The watch records and sends audio to your iPhone for transcription. Summaries need a device with Apple Intelligence.

Does Murmur need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID through the App Store, and Restore purchases brings Pro back on a new device.

Can Murmur identify different speakers?

You can label speakers yourself in the editor. Each speaker gets a colour, appears in exports, and adds up to talk-time bars on iPad and Mac. Murmur does not detect speakers automatically.

What file formats can I export?

Plain text (with optional speakers, timestamps and summary), Markdown, SRT subtitles and M4A audio. You can also copy the text to the clipboard.

Does one purchase cover all my devices?

Yes. Murmur is a universal purchase. Pro bought with your Apple ID works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Murmur Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
