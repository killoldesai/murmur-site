On-device vs cloud transcription

Two ways to turn speech into text. Here is what changes when the model runs on your device instead of a server.

What is the difference between on-device and cloud transcription?

Cloud transcription uploads your audio to a company's servers, which run the speech model and send back text. On-device transcription runs the model on your own phone or computer, so the audio never leaves it. On-device works offline and keeps recordings private; cloud services can offer extras that need a server, such as joining calls.

Privacy

With a cloud service, a copy of your recording exists on someone else's computer, under their retention policy. With on-device transcription the recording and text are only where you put them. That matters for interviews, legal and medical notes, HR conversations and anything under NDA.

Accuracy

Modern on-device models such as Whisper Large v3 Turbo are strong general-purpose recognisers. Audio quality matters more than where the model runs: a close microphone and one speaker at a time beat any setting.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose on-device for confidential recordings, travel and poor connections, and if you do not want another account.
  • Choose cloud if you need a bot to join and record online calls automatically, or you work only on very old hardware.

Frequently asked questions

Is on-device transcription slower?

It depends on the device. Recent iPhones and Apple silicon Macs run Whisper with their Neural Engine and GPU, and there is no upload time.

Can on-device transcription handle long files?

Yes. Murmur converts and transcribes long files in chunks, and queues several files one after another.
