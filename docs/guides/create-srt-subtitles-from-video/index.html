How to create SRT subtitles from a video

Generate a timed SRT caption file from your video on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, without uploading the footage.

What is an SRT file and how do I make one?

An SRT file is a plain-text subtitle format: numbered captions, each with a start and end time and a line of text. To make one, import your video into Murmur, let it transcribe the audio on your device, check the lines, then choose Share, Subtitles to export the SRT.

What an SRT file looks like

Each caption has a number, a time range and the text, separated by blank lines:

1
00:00:04,000 --> 00:00:09,500
Okay, let's start with the Q4 roadmap.

2
00:00:15,000 --> 00:00:21,000
Watch is smaller, and it's the most requested feature.

Steps

  1. Import the video. On iPhone or iPad, tap Import, then Videos to pick from Photos, or Files. On Mac, drag the video into the window.
  2. Let Murmur transcribe it. Murmur extracts the soundtrack and transcribes it with Whisper on your device.
  3. Review the lines. Tap any line to play it, fix words, and split long lines so captions stay short.
  4. Export as Subtitles. Tap Share, choose Subtitles, and save or send the .srt file.
  5. Add it to your video. Upload the SRT in YouTube Studio's subtitles section, or import it into Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

Tips for readable captions

  • Keep each caption to one or two short lines; split long lines in the editor.
  • Add product and people names to the dictionary before importing.
  • Clear audio gives the best timings; reduce music under speech where you can.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SRT supported by YouTube?

Yes. YouTube Studio accepts SRT files as subtitles.

Can I translate subtitles with Murmur?

No. Murmur transcribes in the spoken language. It does not translate.
