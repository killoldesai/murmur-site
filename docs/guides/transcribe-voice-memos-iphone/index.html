How to transcribe Voice Memos on iPhone

A step-by-step guide to turning Voice Memos recordings into text on your iPhone, without uploading them anywhere.

How do I transcribe a Voice Memo on iPhone?

Open the recording in Voice Memos, tap the three-dot menu and choose Share. Pick Murmur if it is listed, or choose Save to Files and then import the file in Murmur. Murmur transcribes it with Whisper on your iPhone, and the text appears as a note you can search, edit and export.

Steps

  1. Install Murmur and download the speech model. Open Murmur, finish onboarding, and download the Whisper model on Wi-Fi. This happens once.
  2. Open the recording in Voice Memos. Tap the memo you want to transcribe.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu, then Share. If Murmur appears in the list of apps, tap it and skip to step 6.
  4. Otherwise choose Save to Files. Save the memo to a folder in On My iPhone or iCloud Drive.
  5. In Murmur, tap Import, then Files. Pick the memo you saved. You can select several at once.
  6. Wait for the transcript. The memo joins Murmur's queue and is transcribed on your iPhone. When it is ready, open the note to read and play it.

Tips for better accuracy

  • Use the Large v3 Turbo model if your iPhone has space; it is Murmur's most accurate option.
  • Add names and jargon to the custom dictionary before transcribing.
  • If a memo is in another language, set the language in Settings or leave it on Auto-detect.
  • Record new memos directly in Murmur so you can drop bookmarks as you go.

Why transcribe on the device?

Voice memos are often personal: ideas, reminders, conversations. Murmur keeps them on your iPhone. The transcription runs locally, there is no account, and the app works offline once the model is downloaded.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Apple's Voice Memos app make transcripts?

Recent versions of iOS can show transcripts in some apps and regions. Murmur adds speaker labels, search across all notes, summaries, custom vocabulary and SRT, Markdown and text export, all on the device.

Can I transcribe many voice memos at once?

Yes. Save them to Files and select them together in Murmur's import screen. They are transcribed one after another.
