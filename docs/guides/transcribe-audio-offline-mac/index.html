How to transcribe audio offline on a Mac

Run Whisper on your Mac's Apple silicon to transcribe recordings with no internet connection and no command line.

How do I transcribe audio on a Mac without uploading it?

Use an app that runs a speech model locally. With Murmur for Mac, download the Whisper model once, then drag audio or video files into the window. Each file joins a queue and is transcribed on your Mac's Apple silicon. Export the result as text, Markdown or SRT.

Steps

  1. Install Murmur for Mac. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.
  2. Download the speech model. Onboarding offers Whisper Large v3 Turbo (about 632 MB) or Small (about 500 MB). You only download it once.
  3. Drag files into the window. Drop one file or many anywhere in Murmur, or click Import.
  4. Watch the queue. Open Queue in the sidebar to see each file's progress and how many times faster than real time it is running. You can pause the queue.
  5. Read and check. Click a note, press Space to play, and use the Up and Down arrows to move between lines.
  6. Export. Click Share and choose Text, Markdown, Subtitles or Audio. Save it or reveal it in Finder.

Recording on the Mac

To record instead, press ⌃⌥R from any app (you can change the shortcut), or use the Murmur item in the menu bar. A small floating window shows the timer. Pick a USB microphone in Settings if you use one.

Do I need the command line?

No. Running Whisper yourself usually means installing Python or building a C++ project and working in Terminal. Murmur packages Whisper (through the WhisperKit framework) as a normal Mac app with a library, editor and export.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Whisper free to use on Mac?

Whisper itself is open source. Murmur is free to download and shows the first two minutes of every note; Murmur Pro unlocks full transcripts and export.

Does Murmur work on Intel Macs?

No. Murmur for Mac needs Apple silicon.
