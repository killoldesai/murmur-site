What is the best offline transcription app for iPhone?

Murmur is an iPhone app that transcribes speech with Whisper on the device, so it works offline and never uploads audio. It records with bookmarks, imports audio and video, summarizes with Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones, and exports text, Markdown or SRT subtitles.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which iPhones can run Murmur?

Murmur runs on recent iPhones. Summaries need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence; transcription works without it.

Can I use Murmur with my Apple Watch?

Yes. Record on the watch and the audio is sent to your iPhone for transcription. New watch recordings are flagged in the library.
